# V2 Implementation-Defined SKG and Lifecycle Construction

## Status boundary

This document records new V2 construction choices. It does not restate, amend,
or attribute these mechanics to a filing.

The following constraints are provisionally attributed by repository
traceability records to an absent primary final specification. They remain
`AI_PROPOSED_AWAITING_APPROVAL`, not authenticated filed wording or a blanket
owner-approved design:

- SKG is the constitutional authority substrate with seven exact content
  classes, and no downstream engine may override its authority encoding.
- Lifecycle governance, obsolescence modelling, and supersession remain
  subordinate to the complete 3P Core.
- The lifecycle layer models transition beyond current AI paradigms and keeps
  the full lifecycle governance envelope secured.
- The three exact lifecycle engines preserve lawful authority continuity,
  prohibit violent or coercive interaction with higher or lower intelligence
  entities, remain bound to 3P and SKG, and cannot self-authorise or supersede
  governance layers.

## Implementation-defined V2 choices

The following mechanics are `IMPLEMENTATION_DEFINED_V2` repository facts. They
make the provisional constraints mechanically evaluable but do not authenticate
their provenance or substantive truth:

1. One authenticated SKG authority evaluation occurs after Root of Trust and
   before procedural truth.
2. The SKG evaluation requires signed evidence for all seven
   repository-configured content
   classes and produces no authority or execution capability.
3. The SKG record, digest, request fingerprint, state hash, evaluation time,
   3P binding, and no-downstream-override invariant are hash-bound and audited.
4. The three lifecycle engines remain separate. V2 evaluates them in the order
   configured in source, while recording that this sequence is a repository
   order and not authenticated filed runtime order.
5. Lifecycle evaluation occurs inside Governance after ABEGF and before final
   Governance completion, GRC, the Governance token, Domain, Aurion, and the
   execution gate.
6. Every lifecycle evaluation has pre- and post-evaluation 3P boundaries and
   binds the current authenticated SKG record. A passing record must explicitly
   attest transition-beyond-current-AI modelling and the secured full lifecycle
   governance envelope.
7. Lifecycle outputs are prerequisite results only. They cannot grant
   Governance ALLOW, a licence, execution approval, execution authority, a
   token with independent authority, or an effect permit.
8. Missing, malformed, reordered, duplicated, unsigned, stale, mutated, 3P-
   unbound, or SKG-unbound records fail closed and trigger the existing licence
   invalidation path.
9. SKG and lifecycle digests are bound into traversal tokens, the execution
   gate, controlled local effect permits and receipts, the hash chain, and the
   terminal audit.
10. The evaluator schema, evidence-reference schema, PASS/DENY/ESCALATE
    vocabulary, record layout, deterministic ordering, and hash/token mechanics
    are all marked `IMPLEMENTATION_DEFINED_V2_MECHANICS_NOT_FILED_SCHEMA`.

## Deliberately unspecified substantive semantics

V2 does not invent definitions, thresholds, transition algorithms, legal
interpretation rules, or intelligence rankings for obsolescence, supersession,
successor intelligence, post-AI eras, or higher/lower intelligence. Those
substantive determinations require a bounded V2 design-authority decision and,
where applicable, real external evaluators with signed evidence under the
mechanical contracts above. They are not material the repository owner is
presumed to owe.
